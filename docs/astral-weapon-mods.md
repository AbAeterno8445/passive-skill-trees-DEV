## **Astral Weapon Modifiers List**

### **Implicit modifiers:**

- **Longswords:**<br>
  +% damage dealt to enemies within 1.5 tiles.

- **Estocs:**<br>
  Consecutive hits against enemies within 2 tiles of you grants +% tears, up to %.<br>
  Hitting an enemy beyond 2 tiles resets the bonus.

- **Daggers:**<br>
  % chance for hits to deal % more damage.<br>
  Double the chance against targets within 1.5 tiles.

- **Quickblades:**<br>
  +tears for X seconds when hitting an enemy, which stacks up to +Y.<br>
  Double the duration if hitting targets within 1.5 tiles.<br>
  Each stack has its own duration.

- **Spears:**<br>
  +% damage dealt to enemies between 1.5 and 2.5 tiles away from you.<br>
  -% damage dealt to enemies within 1.5 tiles.

- **Tridents:**<br>
  Consecutive hits against enemies beyond 1.5 tiles of you grant +% damage and tears, up to %.<br>
  Hitting an enemy within 1.5 tiles of you resets the bonus.

- **Scythes:**<br>
  Hitting an enemy triggers a circular slash that hits nearby enemies for % of the hit's damage.<br>
  This effect has a X second cooldown.

- **Axes:**<br>
  % chance to cause bleeding for 3 seconds when hitting enemies.<br>
  +% damage with hits against bleeding enemies.

- **Greataxes:**<br>
  Every X hits against each enemy causes them to bleed for 4 seconds.<br>
  +% damage for 2 seconds after hitting a bleeding enemy.<br>
  -% tears.

- **Shortbows:**<br>
  +shot speed.<br>
  % of your shot speed above 1 becomes a tears multiplier, up to +80%.

- **Bows:**<br>
  +shot speed.<br>
  Hits against enemies deal additional damage the farther away they are from you, up to %.

- **Crossbows:**<br>
  -tears.<br>
  +shot speed.<br>
  Your total shot speed becomes a damage multiplier, up to %.
